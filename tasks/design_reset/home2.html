Three rules. Written down. Repeated.

No stories, no tips, no conviction bets. A stock is held because it ranks, and sold because it stopped.

01

Own strength, by rank

Each portfolio ranks its universe — the NSE 500 or the Nifty 250 — by measured momentum and holds the top of the list, equal-weighted, capped at 7.5% a position.

02

Exit by rule, not by nerve

Positions leave when they fall out of rank or breach a 20% drawdown stop, checked weekly. The exits are written before the entries are made.

03

Repeat, on cadence

Weekly or bi-weekly rebalances on a fixed calendar, executed from a daily 16:30 IST pipeline. The system runs whether the mood is greed or fear.

We publish the ideas we killed.

Every one of these was built, backtested, and rejected on the evidence. That is how you know the survivors earned their place.

  • Volatility targetingDynamic sizing disrupts the momentum signal it means to protect.
  • Longer lookbacks (9, 12 mo)Underperform the 6-month window on NSE equities.
  • Volume-weighted scoringDollar-volume and OBV blends both trail pure momentum.
  • Profit-hold exit filtersFreeze the portfolio and kill the rotation that drives returns.
  • Re-entry confirmationDelays recovery after corrections; drawdowns explode.
  • Large-caps onlySafer, but leaves roughly 13 points of CAGR in mid-caps.

These systems have drawn down 30–39% in backtests and will draw down again. We do not predict markets; we control position caps (7.5%), stops (20%), and cadence, and we write all three into the rules.
