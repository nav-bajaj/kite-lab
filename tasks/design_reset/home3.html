Three rules run everything.

Chalked on the board, kept in code. Nothing else is allowed to vote.

RULE 1

Own strength, by rank

Rank the universe (NSE 500 or Nifty 250) by measured momentum. Hold the top of the list, equal-weighted, 7.5% cap a name. A stock is held because it ranks, and for no other reason.

RULE 2

Exit by rule, not nerve

Out of rank, or through a 20% drawdown stop: either way, the exit was written before the entry. Discipline is what remains when conviction runs out.

RULE 3

Repeat, on cadence

Signals rebuild after every close at 16:30 IST; rebalances land weekly or bi-weekly on a fixed calendar. Greed, fear, and boredom notwithstanding.

Struck from the board.

A system you can trust names what it discarded. Each of these was built, backtested, and struck off on the evidence.

  • Volatility targetingDynamic sizing disrupts the momentum signal it means to protect.
  • Longer lookbacks (9, 12 months)Underperform the 6-month window on NSE equities.
  • Volume-weighted scoringDollar-volume and OBV blends both trail pure momentum.
  • Profit-hold exit filtersFreeze the portfolio and kill the rotation that drives returns.
  • Re-entry confirmation windowsDelay recovery after corrections; drawdowns explode.
  • Large-caps only (Nifty 100)Safer, but leaves roughly 13 points of CAGR in mid-caps.

Risk, in plain chalk.

These systems have drawn down 30 to 39 percent in backtests, and will draw down again. Momentum pays a premium because holding it through those months is hard. We are probabilistic, never predictive: we do not call markets, we write the controls into the rules.
