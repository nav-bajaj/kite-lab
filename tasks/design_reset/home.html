CLAUSE I

The mechanism

Three rules, held to in writing
1.1

Own strength, by rank.

Every portfolio ranks its universe (the NSE 500 or the Nifty 250) by measured momentum and holds the top of the list, equal-weighted. No stories, no tips: a stock is held because it ranks, and for no other reason.

1.2

Exit by rule, not by nerve.

Positions leave when they fall out of rank or breach a 20% drawdown stop. The exits are written before the entries are made. Discipline is what remains when conviction runs out.

1.3

Repeat, on cadence.

Signals rebuild after every close at 16:30 IST; rebalances land weekly or bi-weekly to a fixed calendar. The system runs whether the mood is greed or fear; that is the point of writing it down.

CLAUSE II

The rejected ledger

Research is what you refuse

A system you can trust names what it discarded.

Every idea below was built, backtested, and rejected on the evidence. The record of failures is part of the product; it is how you know the survivors earned their place.

Tested & rejected Rejected
  • Volatility targetingDynamic sizing disrupts the momentum signal it means to protect.
  • Longer lookbacks (9, 12 months)Underperform the 6-month window on NSE equities.
  • Volume-weighted scoringDollar-volume and OBV blends both trail pure momentum.
  • Profit-hold exit filtersFreeze the portfolio and kill the rotation that drives returns.
  • Re-entry confirmation windowsDelay recovery after corrections; drawdowns explode.
  • Large-caps only (Nifty 100)Safer, but leaves roughly 13 points of CAGR in mid-caps.
CLAUSE III

Risk, in plain terms

Probabilistic, never predictive

These systems have drawn down 30 to 39 percent in backtests, and will draw down again. Momentum pays a premium because holding it through those months is hard. We do not predict markets. We control what is controllable and write it into the rules:

20%Drawdown stop on every position, checked weekly against its running peak.
7.5%Hard cap per position; equal-weight sizing, no conviction bets.
1/N25-stock portfolios — diversified enough to survive a wrong pick.
−50%Defensive Blend cuts allocation in half when the regime signal turns bearish.
