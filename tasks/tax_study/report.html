Tax Study — Production Strategies (v2)

Per-trade FIFO Indian capital-gains tax, 30 bps slippage, forced-sale-for-tax simulation, NIFTY 50 B&H benchmark.

Model:
  • Tax rates: STCG 20% (≤365d hold) · LTCG 12.5% above ₹1.25L FY exemption (>365d hold). Current Indian law (post July 2024), applied throughout.
  • Lot accounting: FIFO. Effective prices include the 30 bps backtest slippage on both buys and sells.
  • Loss carry-forward: 8 financial years. STCL offsets ST or LT gains (FIFO across CF queue). LTCL offsets only LT gains.
  • Tax timing: single annual debit on Apr 1 of each FY for the prior year's gains. Final partial-FY tax (paid after equity series end) is provisioned on the last equity day so deferred liability is reflected.
  • Forced sale: assumes cash < tax owed (fully-invested strategies). Drag = tax × 0.003 / 0.997 ≈ 0.301% of tax. Forced-sale realized P&L not propagated to next FY (bounded error, see Caveats).
  • Equity curve scale: multiplicative — post-tax PV compounds at strategy rate from a smaller base.
  • Initial capital: ₹10L per strategy. LTCG exemption ₹1.25L per FY is meaningful early (~12.5% of starting capital) and dilutes at scale.

CAGR comparison

Takeaways
  • Tax drag is real but doesn't neutralise alpha. Active strategies pay 8–10× more drag in bps (520–700 bp) than B&H (66 bp), yet still deliver 2.7–3.4× higher post-tax CAGR.
  • STCG-heavy strategies pay the most. TL25 v3 (708 bp drag) has effectively zero LTCG — biweekly entry + weekly rank-exit churns positions inside the 12-month bucket. OM25 v3 (520 bp) holds longer because the 20-rank exit buffer + drawdown stop give positions room to mature.
  • Loss carry-forward is doing real work. All 4 strategies use STCL carry-forward at least once over the 16y window; OM25 v3's FY2019-20 ₹7.34L STCL fully offsets FY2020-21's STCG.
  • The ₹1.25L LTCG exemption diminishes fast. In FY2014-15 it shelters the entire LTCG. By FY2024-25 it's a rounding error on a ₹37M tax bill.
  • Forced-sale slippage is essentially noise at 30 bps. Adds ~2 bp/year of drag. The model's simplification (not propagating forced-sale gains to next FY) is well below this.

Caveats — read before using these numbers
  • Backtest warm-start vs cold-start. The 2009-start backtests are sitting on years of accumulated positions at the dashboard's live-window start. OM25 v3 and TL25 v3 live-window CAGRs inside this report exceed the dashboard's published numbers (~6 pp and 1.8 pp respectively) for this reason. L6 v2 and COMBO live-window CAGRs reconcile with the dashboard ±100 bps.
  • Forced-sale realized P&L not propagated. When the engine forces a sale on Apr 1 to pay tax, the realized gain from that sale should logically feed the *next* FY's tax base. We don't model this. The error is bounded: forced sales target the smallest open positions (least appreciated); their realized gains average ~10% of the cash raised; the added tax is ~20% × 10% = 2% of tax owed, i.e. a few bps of CAGR per year.
  • Annual debit, not quarterly advance tax. Real Indian tax is paid in 4 advance-tax installments during the FY, not a lump sum on Apr 1. Our annual model is slightly optimistic for the taxpayer (extra ~6 months of compounding on the tax money). Order-of-magnitude correct.
  • No surcharge or cess. The 20% STCG and 12.5% LTCG rates exclude surcharge (10–37% on tax, depending on income slab) and 4% health-and-education cess. Real effective rates for high-income investors are ~28% STCG / ~17.5% LTCG. Adjust upward accordingly.
  • No money-market return on idle cash. The strategies' cash balance earns 0 in this model. In reality, cash sits in liquid funds at ~6%. COMBO Defensive (which holds 50% cash in bear regime) is most affected — its pre-tax CAGR is understated by ~50 bps.
  • Initial capital is ₹10L. The ₹1.25L LTCG exemption is 12.5% of starting capital — large early, negligible at scale. A ₹1Cr starting investor would see effectively zero exemption benefit. Drag numbers scale roughly with starting capital above ₹50L.
  • Survivorship bias possible. Not audited — the price panel (nse500_data_merged) may include "today's NSE 500" backfilled rather than point-in-time membership.
