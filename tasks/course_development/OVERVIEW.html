Marketworks Market State Lab · exploratory study

Read the market. Build your dashboard.

In six focused hours, a young Indian investor learns to read market state, understand basic quantitative logic, and build a local daily dashboard with AI. The promise is competence, not returns.

3 days 6 modules 6 total hours 6 applied artifacts 1 local dashboard

The learner

Following the market. Not yet reading beneath it.

Age 22–28. Has a mutual-fund SIP, recognizes the Nifty and a few stocks, and uses AI chat and search—but cannot yet connect index moves, participation, leadership, and price patterns.

Starts here
“The Nifty is up, but I do not know whether the market underneath is healthy.”
Graduates here
“I can read trend, breadth, and leadership—and run a dashboard that shows its data and rules.”

The emotional outcome: less overwhelmed by daily noise; more precise about what the evidence does and does not say.

The graduate profile

What can the learner actually do at the end?

Completion means the learner can produce and defend a daily market read—not simply recognize terms or finish a playlist. Every goal leaves evidence.

01Universe map

Define the market before reading it

Map India’s listed-equity landscape, explain why Marketworks begins with a dated Nifty 500-derived universe, and distinguish index weighting from stock participation.

02Five-line market note

Read participation, not just direction

Use the share of stocks above key moving averages and advance–decline evidence to distinguish broad participation from a narrow rally, then write a dated summary.

03Signal anatomy card

Think like a beginner quant

Turn an intuition into an explicit metric or signal with a universe, lookback, calculation, threshold, and failure case. Distinguish a description, screen, forecast, and trading rule.

04Criteria + shortlist

Measure leadership and spot patterns

Read relative strength, simple breakout landmarks, and three program-detected chart structures—triangle, rectangle/base, and bull flag—using numerical criteria plus visual overlays.

05Running dashboard

Build a useful tool with AI

Use Codex or Claude Code to create a local dashboard that calculates market trend, breadth, sector leadership, stock relative strength, and pattern screens from approved data.

06Audit + daily routine

Challenge the machine’s output

Check data freshness, coverage, calculations, missing values, and edge cases; separate observation from inference; and run a repeatable ten-minute market-reading routine.

Local only

No broker connection, credentials, demat details, personal holdings, or trade execution.

Approved data

Use a provided sample or appropriately licensed end-of-day data with source and as-of date.

Transparent rules

Universe, lookbacks, thresholds, missing-data behavior, and limitations stay visible.

Observe, do not advise

Named stocks are screen outputs for further study—not recommendations, targets, or forecasts.

Trust boundary

Conditions, not instructions.

The course is designed to increase learner agency without disguising education as personalized advice.

Educational scope

No personalized advice, named buy/sell calls, price targets, guarantees, or trade execution.

Dated evidence

Market views carry an as-of date and distinguish observation, inference, and uncertainty.

Safe AI use

No credentials or personal portfolio data; local observation only; definitions, limitations, and AI assistance disclosed.

Compliance review

Named-security screens, performance language, data rights, and public dashboard templates require review before launch.
