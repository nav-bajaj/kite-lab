Part 01 · Vocabulary

“The market” is not one list

Five different objects are routinely compressed into one casual phrase. Pulling them apart is the first quantitative habit.

Company
The underlying business: its factories, employees, software, brands, contracts, debts, cash flows, assets, and legal obligations.
Security
A financial claim. A company’s ordinary equity share is one kind. ETFs, debt securities, preference shares, REITs, and InvITs are others.
Exchange
A regulated venue where eligible securities are admitted and traded. NSE and BSE both operate equity segments, and many companies trade on both.
Index
A rules-based measurement of a selected basket, with eligibility, selection, weighting, calculation, and review rules.
Research universe
The explicitly defined set of securities to which we apply a question or calculation.
The broker-search trap

“Available in my broker app” is a product search result, not a reproducible research-universe definition.

India has more than one recognized stock exchange. A company may have securities admitted on multiple exchanges, so NSE and BSE company counts cannot simply be added. A broker search can also mix ordinary shares with other instruments and segments.

“Everything listed” sounds comprehensive, but it mixes securities with very different liquidity, trading frequency, listing history, market capitalization, information availability, and data quality. A beginner can mistake a low share price for a cheap valuation or a large percentage move for meaningful information without noticing how little value traded.

The purpose of narrowing is not to declare the excluded companies bad. It is to create a coherent first laboratory.

A universe follows a question

Largest companies, broad participation, and microcap liquidity are three different questions. They should not inherit the same denominator by accident.

Part 03 · The index

What the Nifty 500 actually is

The official name is Nifty 500. “NSE 500” is product shorthand; it is not the index’s official name.

In course material, Nifty 500 means the official index. Marketworks Nifty 500-derived universe means the dated constituent and data snapshot used by a calculation. That wording keeps exchange, index, and product dataset separate.

The official methodology first forms an eligible NSE universe. Its current criteria include:

  • Indian domicile and NSE trading or permitted-to-trade status
  • Eligible security types and specified exclusions
  • Minimum free float or free-float market capitalization
  • Trading on at least 90% of days in the prior six months
  • Average impact cost not greater than 1%
  • A listing-history condition for newly listed securities
  • Top-800 rank on average turnover and full market capitalization
  • Top 500 selected by six-month average full market capitalization

Plain-language summary only. The complete, changeable rules belong to the official Nifty equity-index methodology, March 2026.

What sits inside the 500?

The Nifty 500 brings the first three size layers together. That gives the learner exposure to different company sizes without immediately moving into the longer, less liquid tail.

The index level is free-float market-capitalization weighted. Market capitalization is share price multiplied by shares outstanding. Free float is the portion considered available for public trading rather than tightly held strategic or promoter ownership. Larger free-float companies therefore have more influence on the index return.

Under the current schedule, Nifty 500 reconstitution changes are effective on the last working day of March and September, with additional review possible for events such as schemes, suspensions, and delistings. Membership is data, and it has a date.

The Nifty 500 is not the whole Indian market. It balances coverage, range, investability logic, analytical depth, and a common language.

01Coverage

It captures most of the economically relevant listed market while leaving the unmeasured tail visible.

03Eligibility

Trading frequency, impact cost, free float, security type, and other conditions reduce obvious noise before size selection.

04Cross-section

Roughly five hundred intended members support breadth, relative-strength, sector, and unusual-condition comparisons.

05Shared language

A dated common universe makes calculations reproducible and comparable across learners and product views.

A research universe is not a starter portfolio

  • proof that every constituent is high quality or attractively valued
  • a guarantee that every security suits every investor or order size
  • the entire Indian listed market or the full microcap tail
  • a permanent historical list free from survivorship bias
  • a recommendation to buy, hold, or sell any constituent
Part 05 · Two market views

The index can rise while most stocks fall

A weighted index measures the movement of capital-weighted members. Breadth measures how many covered stocks participated.

Both statements are true: the weighted index rose and eight of ten stocks fell. Direction and participation answer different questions.

Marketworks often counts each eligible, covered stock once for breadth, even though the official Nifty 500 index gives larger free-float companies more influence. Neither view is “the real market.” Together they reveal more than either alone.

Part 06 · Data discipline

Membership is not the same as usable coverage

A metric may need more history than every current member possesses. The denominator must disclose that difference.

A current Nifty 500 list used to calculate today’s breadth is a current cross-section. Projecting that same list backward for ten years omits companies that disappeared and includes current members before they joined. That creates survivorship and membership bias.

An official index may target 500 companies while a dashboard has usable history for fewer on a specific date. A dated official factsheet can also temporarily publish a different security count during maintenance or a corporate action. Preserve the official dated count, check official notices, and never silently force it to 500.
