marketworks. brand guide · colour system · proposal 01

One green. White ground. Ink first.

The palette picker retires. Marketworks runs a single colour system with one light theme and one dark companion — the brand is the green, the ground stays white for readability and pop, and colour arrives through elements: buttons, chips, lines, washes. Never through tinted page bands.

Ground is whitePure white page; one near-white band tone for section rhythm. No creams, no palette washes.
Text is ink + one greyAlmost-black ink for content, a single grey for secondary. Colour is never body text.
Green is the brandOne anchor green in the saturated-luminous band (~5:1 on white), with a vivid, a tint and a deep step.

Marketworks lives in the overlap of Finance × Technology × Editorial Research. Every element should sit in that overlap: numbers treated with editorial care, engineering treated as a brand surface, writing treated as a product. The system above is judged against this before anything ships.

Foundations

Grounds and inks. Contrast badges are computed live against the active theme — they can never go stale.

The green

Four steps, four jobs. The anchor is text-safe and carries the wordmark and every primary action; the vivid draws lines and large marks; the tint washes hover fields and chips; the deep grounds the one drench moment per page and headlines on tint.

marketworks. wordmark + button roles, on the live theme

Supporting accents

Five hues, kept on a leash. Each works as a clay-style triad — tint surface · vivid mark · deep text — and an element takes ONE hue, all three tones. Accents never colour body copy and never tint a full page band.

Roles: sun — highlights, education, "new" · sky — links to externals, info, benchmark data · coral — warnings, drawdowns, destructive actions · purple — the editorial voice: pull-quotes, research callouts, /library moments (a brand heirloom) · lime — the highlighter: annotations, marks, energy; its vivid is never text.

Data colours

Charts get two series inks, validated for colour-vision safety on both themes (CVD ΔE 21+, contrast ≥3:1). Direction: green means up — the brand accepts the finance convention — so near charts, green is reserved for meaning and the UI around a chart stays ink and grey.

Rhythm: bands and cards, one grammar

The homepage alternates full-width bands with carded clusters. White page → near-white band → cards on white → one deep-green drench near the close. The miniature below runs entirely on the role tokens; toggle the theme and it re-derives.

The one drench moment

Deep green, once per page, near the close. It reads as the signature because it is scarce.

Text on the system

Momentum, held to in writing.

Ink carries the reading. The lede stays ink; hierarchy comes from size, not from fading everything to grey.

Body copy may use the grey when it is genuinely secondary — supporting notes, method lines, captions. One grey, used deliberately, beats four greys used by accident.

Caption grey, 12.5px, above a hairline. The lightest text tone that still passes 4.5:1.
