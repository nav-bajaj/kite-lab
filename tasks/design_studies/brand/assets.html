Surfaces: cards

Six card treatments on the system. Default is the hairline card; the others are deliberate upgrades, each with a named job. Hover lift is gated to fine pointers and 180ms ease-out.

Hairline card

The default container. One border, no shadow at rest, lift on hover where the card is a link.

border 1px --line · r 14

Lifted card

Shadow instead of border — for the one card per section that carries the headline number or the primary path.

--shadow · no border

Wash card

Green-tint field with deep-green ink. Callouts, "start here" moments, the calm version of emphasis.

bg --green-tint · ink --green-deep

Mono-meta card

A Plex Mono header row turns any card into an instrument readout — the loop-24 "more mono" move for data cards.

header: mono 11px --green

Dither-edge card

A one-line dither strip on the top edge. The texture signature at card scale, used sparingly.

8×8 bayer strip · --green-tint

Keyline card

A 2px vivid keyline marks membership in a set — the accent-triad carrier at card scale.

keyline 2px --green-vivid

Surfaces: bands

Full-width section treatments, in the order a homepage would breathe them: white → soft → textured → one drench near the close.

Soft band. The near-white field for quiet content — process, methodology, explanations.

bg --bg-soft

Paper band. The chart-paper grid as a section ground; content cards sit directly on it.

grid 36px --line

Dither band. The Bayer texture as a transition moment between white sections.

bayer 8×8 fade

The drench. Deep green, once per page, near the close. Scarcity is what makes it the signature.

bg --green-deep

The dither patterns

One 8×8 Bayer matrix, five densities — a reusable SVG pattern set. Dither is the brand's texture: it is literally data resolved into an image, which is the product. Use in green-tint on white, or pale-on-deep in the drench.

Motion tokens

Adopted from Emil Kowalski's animation standards: transform and opacity only, strong curves, UI under 300ms, ambient marketing motion slow and interruptible, reduced-motion gentler not zero, hover gated to fine pointers.

Press feedback

scale(0.97) on :active, 160ms ease-out. Every pressable element.

Entrance

opacity 0 → 1 with translateY(8px) → 0, 300ms ease-out. Never from scale(0).

Signals updated

Reveal

clip-path inset sweep, 600ms ease-out — for charts and media drawing in on scroll.

rules: transform/opacity only · no ease-in · no scale(0) · stagger 30–80ms · UI <300ms
gates: @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) → static fields, keep fades · @media (hover:hover) → hover motion
