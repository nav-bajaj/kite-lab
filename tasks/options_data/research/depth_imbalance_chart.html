Depth imbalance vs 1-minute candles

Marketworks options engine — real captured bars, whole-book imbalance = (bidQty − askQty)/(bidQty + askQty)
hover the chart for per-minute values
top: 1-min OHLC + traded volume (faint)
bottom: avg depth imbalance, −1…+1 (green = bid-heavy book, red = ask-heavy)

Times are IST. Imbalance is the tick-weighted minute average of the whole-book resting quantity skew — the “Total” row of the market-depth window, not just the visible 5 levels. Bars with no imbalance (none here) would simply show no histogram column.
