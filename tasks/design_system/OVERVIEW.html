Initiative Map · design_system

One brand contract, applied to every surface.

A canonical design system (~/marketworks-design, package @marketworks/design) and its application across the Marketworks web product — social asset templates, the public library, the insights dashboard, the landing + auth, and the authenticated app — on one token contract, light and dark.

Opened 2026-06-03 · Shipped 2026-06-05 · target viewer: Karan (mobile-first)

Why
The product looked ad-hoc; the brand wasn't load-bearing.

Each surface had grown its own styles. The design system makes a single token contract the source of truth, validated on real surfaces in a Pencil brand guide before engineering, then mirrored into the app so it ships without a cross-repo dependency.
