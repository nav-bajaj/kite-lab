Ebbs & flows

Crowding mean-reverts — it swells over months then unwinds, rather than trending. It is a slow oscillator, not a straight line.

Bull markets crowd

The shaded (crowded) stretches sit mostly inside strong up-moves in the price panel — momentum crowds when it is working, which is why it isn't a sell signal.

Spikes = theme manias

The tallest spikes align with narrow, frenzied leadership — early-2023 and mid-2024 — when a single theme dominated the momentum list.
